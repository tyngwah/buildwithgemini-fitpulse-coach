# My agent: FitPulse Coach
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps athletes and everyday fitness enthusiasts track workouts, plan nutrition, and reach goals with a catalog of exercises and meal plans.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: Fitness goals, personal records (PRs), dietary preferences/allergies, injury history, and past workout sessions
- Tools: Log workout sets/reps, look up exercises, query nutritional info, calculate heart rate training zones
- Catalog/UI: Exercise movement library and meal plan recipes rendered as rich A2UI cards and training summary tables
- Image gen: Motivational workout milestones, visual meal plating ideas, and achievement badges
- Sandbox: Macro/calorie calculations, TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure), and volume progression math

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch (pick what fits): code sandbox for nutrition/progression calculations, Cloud Trace for latency monitoring
